AgexFi (AGEX)

Official Smart Contract & Technical Documentation

AgexFi (AGEX) is a community-focused digital token deployed on BNB Smart Chain.

The project is built around a simple concept: an accessible digital token supporting genuine on-chain trading, direct wallet transfers, transparent blockchain activity, and community participation.

AGEX uses a straightforward BEP-20 smart contract with a fixed supply and no complex token-level trading restrictions.

Smart Contract Characteristics

The deployed AGEX token contract includes:

- Fixed initial supply of 100 million AGEX.
- No external minting function.
- No built-in transfer taxes.
- No token-level blacklist mechanism.
- No transfer-pause mechanism.
- No special transfer privileges for project-controlled wallets.
- Standard ERC-20 transfer and approval functionality.
- No built-in vesting or mandatory token-allocation mechanism.

The entire token supply was minted to the designated initial recipient at deployment.

Subsequent token transfers and wallet balances are publicly verifiable on BNB Smart Chain.

Token Distribution and Transparency

The AGEX smart contract does not enforce predefined allocation percentages, vesting schedules, or token lock-up requirements.

Project-controlled wallets operate independently of the token contract and follow the same token transfer rules as other holders.

Some token holdings remain concentrated in project-controlled wallets. These holdings and their transactions can be independently inspected on BscScan.

No claim of locked liquidity or locked token allocations is made without separate, verifiable evidence.
